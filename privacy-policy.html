Privacy Policy for Cosmic Strike

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This policy explains what information the Cosmic Strike game for iPhone and Android ("the app", "we", "us") handles, why, and the choices you have. Cosmic Strike is made by Bhavesh Bhoyar.

The short version

  • You don't create an account, and we don't ask for your name, email or contact details.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. We have no servers and never receive them.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage information to show and measure ads. If you live in the EEA, UK or Switzerland you are asked for consent first. On iPhone, Apple also asks whether the app may track you.
  • The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to see how the game is played, such as runs started and finished, scores and coins earned and spent. You can turn this off in the app's settings.
  • If you use Game Center (iPhone) or Google Play Games (Android), your scores and achievements are sent to Apple or Google.
  • In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your phone so the game works between sessions:

  • Game progress: coins, level and XP, upgrades, unlocked ships, best scores, achievements and campaign progress
  • Settings: sound, music, haptics, controls and other preferences
  • Ad and purchase records: how many ad rewards you've claimed today, when the last between-run ad was shown, and which purchases have been delivered
  • Prompt records: when you were last asked to rate the game, and which update you chose to see later

This information stays on your device. Apart from the progress summary and gameplay events described in sections 3 and 5, it is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot access it. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset progress → Erase in the app, or by deleting the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, to show rewarded ads (which you choose to watch for an in-game reward) and occasional ads between runs. We never show ads during play.

To serve, measure and limit fraud on ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Device information, such as device model, operating system version, language and screen size
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Your device's advertising identifier: on iPhone, the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when Apple asks; on Android, the Android advertising ID, unless you have deleted it in your phone's settings
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and whether you tapped them
  • Diagnostic data, such as app crashes and performance related to ads

Google uses this information as described in its policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices about ads

  • Tracking (iPhone, all regions). When you first open the app, iOS asks whether Cosmic Strike may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared and you see ads that are less personalised. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Advertising ID (Android, all regions). You can delete or reset your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads). Once it is deleted, it is not shared and you see ads that are less personalised.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). Before any ad is requested, Google's consent form asks whether your data may be used for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Remove ads. Buying Remove Ads or the Supporter Pack turns off between-run ads, and rewards are granted without watching an ad.

3. Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and improve it (for example, which stages are too hard, how long runs last, and which screens are used), the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google LLC. It sends Google:

  • Gameplay events, such as starting and finishing a run, the mode and stage played, score, wave reached, time played, bosses defeated, and whether a continue was used
  • In-game economy events, such as coins earned and spent, upgrades and ships bought, and achievements claimed
  • Screen views, such as opening the shop or settings
  • Ad and purchase events, such as whether a rewarded ad was watched, and which product was bought with its price and currency (never your payment details)
  • A summary of your progress: player level, selected ship, highest campaign stage, and whether you own Remove Ads
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, app version, and an app instance ID that Firebase creates for this installation
  • Your approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address
  • Your device's advertising identifier, under the same conditions as in section 2

This data is not linked to your name, email, Apple Account or Google Account, and we never see it about any individual player. We see only combined reports. Google keeps it for a limited period, set in our Google Analytics settings (at most 14 months). Advertising features in Google Analytics use your data only if ads may use it: on iPhone, only if you allow tracking; and in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, only if you consent to ads in Google's consent form.

More about how Google handles this data: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Your choice about analytics. You can turn analytics off at any time in the app under Settings → Share usage data. Once it is off, no analytics data is sent from your device.

4. In-app purchases

On iPhone, purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store, under Apple's privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). On Android, purchases are processed by Google through Google Play, under Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app receives only a confirmation of which product was bought, so it can deliver it and restore it on your other devices. We do not receive your name, Apple Account or Google Account details, or payment details.

5. Leaderboards and achievements (Game Center and Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Game Center on iPhone or Google Play Games on Android, the app sends your scores (best Endless and Daily Challenge scores, and campaign stars) and your achievement progress to Apple or Google, so they can show them on leaderboards and in your achievements. Your Game Center or Play Games name and picture may be shown to other players on leaderboards, according to your settings in those services.

Apple and Google handle this under their own privacy policies (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ and https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app does not receive your email address, and we do not store or see your Game Center or Play Games profile. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent. You can sign out at any time in your phone's Game Center settings or in the Google Play Games app.

6. Ratings and updates

Now and then, after a good run, the app may ask you to rate it using Apple's or Google's own rating window. Your rating and review go straight to the App Store or Google Play; we don't see who left them. When the app starts, it asks the App Store (with your country, so it checks the right store) or Google Play whether a newer version is available. No personal information is sent with this check.

7. What we don't do

  • We do not run our own servers. The only usage data we receive is the combined Google Analytics reports described in section 3.
  • We do not use crash-reporting services, and we do not combine analytics data with information from other sources to identify you.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

8. Children

Cosmic Strike is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to or limit how it is used.

  • Data stored on your device is under your control: erase it with Reset progress or by deleting the app.
  • For data collected by Google for advertising, use the choices in section 2, or Google's tools at https://myadcenter.google.com.
  • To stop analytics, turn off Settings → Share usage data in the app. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your installation, contact us and we will request deletion from Google.
  • For leaderboard and achievement data, use Apple's or Google's own privacy tools, or sign out of Game Center or Google Play Games.
  • For any other question or request, contact us using the details below.

10. Security

Your progress is stored in the app's private storage on your device, protected by your phone's operating system. Ad requests, analytics data, and leaderboard and achievement data are sent to Google or Apple over encrypted connections.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
