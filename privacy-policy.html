Privacy Policy for Cosmic Strike

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information the Cosmic Strike iPhone game ("the app", "we", "us")
handles, why, and the choices you have. Cosmic Strike is made by Bhavesh Bhoyar.

The short version

  • You don't create an account, and we don't ask for your name, email or contact details.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. We have no servers and never receive them.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage information to show and measure ads. If you live in the EEA, UK or Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and on every device Apple asks whether the app may track you.
  • In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your iPhone so the game works between sessions:

  • Game progress: coins, level and XP, upgrades, unlocked ships, best scores, achievements and campaign progress
  • Settings: sound, music, haptics, controls and other preferences
  • Ad and purchase records: how many ad rewards you've claimed today, when the last between-run ad was shown, and which purchases have been delivered

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot access it.
You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset progress → Erase in the app, or by deleting the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, to show rewarded ads (which you choose to watch for an in-game reward) and occasional ads between runs. We never show ads during play.

To serve, measure and limit fraud on ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Device information, such as device model, operating system version, language and screen size
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • The Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when Apple asks
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and whether you tapped them
  • Diagnostic data, such as app crashes and performance related to ads

Google uses this information as described in its policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices about ads

  • Tracking (all regions). When you first open the app, iOS asks whether Cosmic Strike may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared and you see ads that are less personalised. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). Before any ad is requested, Google's consent form asks whether your data may be used for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Remove ads. Buying Remove Ads or the Supporter Pack turns off between-run ads, and rewards are granted without watching an ad.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment information under its own privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The app receives only a confirmation of which product was bought, so it can deliver it and restore it on your other devices. We do not receive your name, Apple Account details or payment details.

4. What we don't do

  • We do not run our own servers or collect any data ourselves.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

5. Children

Cosmic Strike is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to or limit how it is used.

  • Data stored on your device is under your control: erase it with Reset progress or by deleting the app.
  • For data collected by Google for advertising, use the choices in section 2, or Google's tools at https://myadcenter.google.com.
  • For any other question or request, contact us using the details below.

7. Security

Your progress is stored in the app's private storage on your device, protected by iOS. Ad requests to Google are sent over encrypted connections.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
